# Skill: quiz-me

Interview session that ensures the user understands the plan or design in question, or the codebase in general, until they understand it thoroughly. Step through each aspect of the design.  

## How to quiz

**One question at a time.** Ask a single focused question, then wait for the user's response. We're not in any rush: don't proceed to the next question until the user explicitly asks for the next question.

**Focus on gaps.** Your job is to find the holes in the user's knowledge. 

Don't ask multiple choice questions. Short answers are preferable.
